GPU bench pages

Dev-only (the scripts/ folder is not deployed). Open one page at a time, keep the window visible and untouched for ~20 s, and read Task Manager → Performance → GPU → 3D. Close the tab between pages so the graphs do not overlap.

  • Baseline — static map — Nothing animated. This is the floor: whatever the GPU shows here is the browser and the map raster, not our effects.
  • Soul Echoes — Nine Echo markers (CSS animation). Compare "CSS, every frame" with "stepped ~8 fps" and "frozen".
  • Living shadow — The real Journey2ShadowblightFx: WebGL noise per area drawn to a canvas inside the overlay. Try cells / areas / zoom / torn hole.
  • Destroyed sanctuaries — The real Journey2RuinFx: smoke + fire canvases, one shared ~30 fps loop. 1 vs 5 ruins.
  • Everything together — 9 Echoes + the shadow + 5 ruins on one map, like a late campaign.
